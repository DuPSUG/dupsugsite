# DuPSUG – PSConfEU MiniCon 2026 🚀 - Virtual Booth

The Dutch PowerShell User Group (DuPSUG) is excited to connect with our community in person around PSConfEU MiniCon 2026! We're joining PowerShell enthusiasts together to share knowledge, exchange ideas, and celebrate the community while taking part in a full day of free, live PowerShell content from around the world.

## What is PSConfEU MiniCon?

PSConfEU MiniCon returns on 13 October 2026, offering a full day of free live PowerShell sessions for participants across the globe. With its unique follow-the-sun format, the event brings speakers and attendees together across time zones, from the Pacific through Europe to the Americas.

*Save the date: 13 October 2026*

This year's PSConfEU MiniCon takes place on Tuesday, 13 October 2026. Be sure to reserve your free place and explore the schedule so you can plan your day around the sessions that interest you most.
